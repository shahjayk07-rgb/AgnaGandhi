§ 09 — The future
Some chapters should be written by their owner.

This archive was never meant to last forever.

One day, replace every page here with the website of your own law firm. That will mean this archive has fulfilled its purpose.

agnagandhi.in · a temporary residence · handing over soon

…

Life changes. People change. Paths separate. That is part of being human.

Some stories are meant to continue together. Others quietly become memories that stay with us for the rest of our lives.

Whatever ours became, I have never looked back on it with regret. It was beautiful. It made me laugh. It made me grow. It gave me memories that I will always carry with gratitude.

This website was never built to ask for another chance. It was built because I wanted one small corner of the internet to exist that celebrates the person I came to admire so deeply.

If, years from now, you happen to visit this page again, I hope you don't think about who built it. I hope you think about yourself. About how far you've come. About every difficult day you survived. About every dream that once felt impossibly far away and slowly became your everyday life.

If this little place makes you smile for even a moment, then it has already fulfilled its purpose.

Take care of yourself.
Sleep when you're tired.
Eat properly.
Celebrate the small victories.
Be kind to yourself on the difficult days.
And never stop believing in the future you've worked so hard to build.

There is just one last thing I never wanted this website to leave unsaid.

I loved what we had.
I love the person you are.

I love you.
